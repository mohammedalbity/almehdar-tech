# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary:** SMB founders, business owners, product managers, and enterprise decision-makers across Saudi Arabia and the GCC seeking professional digital transformation, bespoke web applications, e-commerce infrastructure, and data solutions.
- **Secondary:** End consumers and business clients engaging with client portals, storefronts, and digital services.

## Product Purpose

Al-Mehdar Tec (المحضار تك) transforms business ideas into robust, high-performance digital realities. The company delivers end-to-end digital solutions—from custom websites and secure e-commerce platforms to bespoke software architecture, data analytics, and continuous technical support—empowering clients to lead in an increasingly digital economy.

## Positioning

Integrated digital engineering under one roof. Unlike fragmented design agencies or offshore code factories, Al-Mehdar Tec combines modern UI/UX design, custom software development, and actionable data analytics with native Arabic-first UX excellence, local market alignment, and enterprise-grade reliability.

## Operating Context

- **Market & Locale:** Saudi Arabia and GCC markets operating in Arabic-first (RTL) and bilingual environments.
- **Client Dynamics:** Professional B2B sales cycles requiring credible proof of delivery, clear technical capabilities, transparent processes, and accessible communication channels (Email, Phone/WhatsApp).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Confirmed Capabilities:**
  - Bespoke website design and frontend development
  - Fully integrated e-commerce solutions with payment gateways and inventory workflows
  - Data management, analytics dashboards, and business intelligence reporting
  - Custom software development, system architecture, and API integrations
  - Digital brand identity and UI/UX design
  - Dedicated 24/7 technical support, infrastructure maintenance, and system updates
- **Technical & Design Constraints:**
  - Framework: Nuxt 4 (`^4.5.2`) + Vue 3 + TypeScript
  - Styling: Tailwind CSS v4 + Vanilla CSS custom properties
  - RTL-first structure with Arabic typography (Cairo font family)
  - Dark mode aesthetic anchored in deep navy tones (`#0a0f2e`, `#141b3d`) with luminous accents (`#4f7cff`, `#00d4ff`, `#7c4dff`)

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Al-Mehdar Tec | المحضار تك
- **Tagline:** WEB . DATA . TECHNOLOGY | حلول تقنية احترافية

## Product Principles

1. **Integrated Craft:** Design, software engineering, and data insights work in unison—never in disconnected silos.
2. **Arabic-First Polish:** Natural, balanced RTL typography and layouts crafted natively, not as an afterthought translation.
3. **Performance & Durability:** Lightweight, fast-loading, clean codebases designed to scale with client growth.
4. **Transparency & Rigor:** Clear project phases, verified deliverables, and disciplined execution.
5. **Continuous Value:** Long-term partnership through maintenance, monitoring, and proactive support.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards for color contrast across all dark background surfaces.
- Semantic HTML5 landmark structure with accessible forms, focus states, and responsive touch targets.
- Smooth reduced-motion consideration for users with motion sensitivity.
